# VolleyStars

A turn-based creature volleyball game that runs in the browser.

## Files
- `index.html` – the page
- `style.css` – how it looks
- `js/creatures.js` – creature names, stats and classes (edit this to balance or add creatures)
- `js/rules.js` – all the game math (serves, passes, spikes, blocks, tiredness, CPU brain)
- `js/game.js` – the screens and match flow
- `assets/` – art

## How to play
1. Pick 3–6 creatures and place them on your court. Press ★ to choose your server.
2. Serve by clicking a spot on the CPU's side.
3. When the ball comes to your creature, click a teammate to pass to it.
   Turn **Set** on (or press S) to set instead of bump.
4. A spiker who receives a set can spike: click a CPU creature or an empty spot to hit at.
5. **Send it over** (or press F) sends an easy free ball. On the 3rd touch you must send it over.
